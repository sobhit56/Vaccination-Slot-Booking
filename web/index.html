❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions and guidelines for your vaccination appointment.

📄 What documents do I need to bring?

Please carry the same government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID) entered during slot booking, along with your printed or digital appointment slip on your mobile.

⏳ What happens if my preferred slot is full?

You can join the Priority Waitlist. When any appointment is cancelled, our system automatically reallocates the slot to waitlisted patients based on clinical priority and senior citizen status.

💉 What is the required gap between Dose 1 & Dose 2?

Clinical rules enforce safety intervals: Covishield requires an 84-day gap (12-16 weeks), Covaxin requires 28 days (4 weeks), and Pfizer requires 21 days (3 weeks).

🛡️ Is there any fee for booking?

No, booking through this portal is completely free. Government-partnered vaccination centers administer approved vaccines under standard national health protocols.
